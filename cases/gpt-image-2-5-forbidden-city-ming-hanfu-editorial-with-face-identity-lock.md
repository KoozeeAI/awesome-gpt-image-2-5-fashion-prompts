High-end editorial fashion photograph, magazine cover quality (Vogue / Harper's Bazaar / ELLE), photorealistic — not an illustration, not 3D render, not AI-glossy.

[your face image] is the identity reference. The subject is the adult woman in [your face image]: her facial features and bone structure must match EXACTLY — eye spacing, eye shape, nose bridge, nose tip, lip shape, jawline, cheekbone structure, chin. Same person, zero identity drift. Preserve her light purple eyes, pink-toned lips, and the small mole on her left cheek. Skin texture must be truly photorealistic: visible pores, fine skin detail, natural tonal variation, slightly imperfect real skin. Do NOT over-smooth, do NOT apply an Instagram-filter beauty look, no plastic skin.

SCENE — WINTER AT THE FORBIDDEN CITY: a long red-walled corridor of the Beijing Forbidden City in winter. Vermilion painted walls, tall red lacquered pillars, intricate carved wooden latticed windows, painted wooden beams above with strong one-point perspective depth down the corridor. Fine snowflakes are gently falling through the crisp winter air. Remnant snow on the eaves and stone steps. The scene is completely clean and empty — no tourists, no modern signage, no watermarks, no text anywhere in the image.

WARDROBE — MING DYNASTY HEAVY-INDUSTRY COUTURE: opulent multi-layered Ming Dynasty ceremonial Hanfu, gold-heavy and dignified. A crisp, visible white standing inner collar providing a clean boundary at the neck. Over it, a structured duijin ao (jacket) with wide sleeves. On top, a heavy xiapei / pibo stole structure held at the center by a large ornate yajin ornament. Main fabric is real zhijin jin (woven gold brocade) with palpable fiber texture. Embroidery is heavy industry goldwork — couched gold thread, panjin, dense tassels, phoenix motifs. She wears a phoenix crown (fengguan) with gold filigree, pearls and precious stones. Gold ornaments and gemstones must have realistic specular highlights, bright but never blown out. Embroidery textures razor-sharp and tactile.

PHOTOGRAPHY & CINEMATOGRAPHY: medium shot, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, eyes perfectly sharp. Natural winter daylight supplemented with soft professional fill light. Rich cinematic color grading: the vermilion red of the walls and the gold of the attire are clean, distinct and saturated, never muddy or crushed. Deep, textured, expensive look with lifted shadows, soft highlights, gentle bloom and fine film grain. Natural lens compression, subtle humid haze, imperfect real-camera texture.

COMPOSITION: vertical 9:16 magazine cover layout. Clean composition with deliberate empty negative space at the top of the frame for typography. Calm, regal, level gaze into the lens. No torn paper, no hand-drawn effects, no collage, no text or logos.
