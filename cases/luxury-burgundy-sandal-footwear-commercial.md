# Luxury Burgundy Sandal Footwear Commercial

A curated AI fashion prompt for Shoes · Reference-locked luxury sandal cinematic footwear advertising with strict product consistency · Video.

## Prompt

The luxury is here
Made with GPT IMAGE 2.5 and Seedance 2.5 on @PixVerse 
Prompt:
Using the supplied reference image as the exact product reference, create an ultra-premium cinematic fashion advertising film for the same fictional burgundy luxury sandal.

PRODUCT CONSISTENCY:
The sandal must remain EXACTLY the same throughout the entire sequence. Preserve its deep burgundy/oxblood color, oversized sculptural silhouette, architectural platform sole, asymmetric straps, dark-chrome hardware, stitching, embossed geometric emblem, proportions, materials and construction. Never redesign, morph, duplicate or replace the product.

CONCEPT:
A futuristic luxury footwear campaign introducing the sandal as an iconic fashion object.

The film should feel like a global premium fashion advertisement — sophisticated, cinematic, minimal, bold and expensive.

VISUAL STYLE:
Ultra-photorealistic luxury commercial.
Dark futuristic fashion runway.
Glossy black reflective floor.
Deep charcoal architectural environment.
Subtle atmospheric haze.
Burgundy light accents.
Controlled studio lighting.
High-contrast cinematic exposure.
Premium editorial color grading.
Realistic leather and metal reflections.
Extremely clean visual composition.

CAMERA & MOTION:
Use constantly evolving premium commercial cinematography:

* Extreme wide establishing shot
* Low-angle hero shot
* Slow cinematic dolly-in
* Smooth lateral tracking shot
* Macro product detail shot
* Controlled 90-degree orbit
* Low-to-high reveal
* Fast but elegant push-in
* Slow-motion three-quarter rotation
* Extreme close-up of leather texture
* Close-up of dark-chrome hardware
* Final symmetrical hero composition

All camera movement must be perfectly stabilized, intentional and physically realistic.

EDITING:
Create sophisticated modern commercial editing with precise cuts synchronized to the music.

Use:

* Speed ramps
* Elegant match cuts
* Subtle whip transitions
* Motion-blur transitions
* Micro zoom transitions
* Seamless angle changes
* Short rhythmic cuts
* Slow-motion moments for product details
* Clean cinematic fades
* Brief black-frame impact transitions

Never use cheap, excessive or distracting effects.

KINETIC TYPOGRAPHY / SUBTITLES:
Integrate premium animated typography directly into the commercial.

Use minimal elegant typography with modern editorial fashion-advertising aesthetics.

Example on-screen text sequence:

“BUILT TO BE SEEN”

then:

“SCULPTED IN BURGUNDY”

then:

“FORM. POWER. PRECISION.”

then:

“THE NEW ICON”

Typography should animate with subtle tracking expansion, smooth upward movement, masked reveals, kinetic positioning and precise timing with the soundtrack.

Text should appear naturally within negative space and NEVER cover important parts of the sandal.

Use clean premium white/off-white typography with extremely subtle burgundy accents.

Do not use recognizable real-world brand typography or logos.

VOICEOVER:
Add a deep, sophisticated cinematic voiceover with a calm confident delivery.

Voiceover:

“Some designs are made to be worn.

Others are made to be remembered.

Sculpted with precision.
Defined by presence.

This is more than a sandal.

This is the new icon.”

The voice should sound like a premium international fashion campaign: restrained, confident, cinematic and sophisticated.

AUDIO DESIGN:
Create a complete luxury commercial soundscape.

Begin with a deep atmospheric cinematic tone.

Gradually introduce:

* Deep sub-bass
* Elegant electronic pulse
* Minimal fashion-show percussion
* Subtle metallic impacts
* Soft whooshes during camera movements
* Cinematic risers before major reveals
* Sharp impact sound during typography transitions
* Subtle leather/material foley
* Spacious reverb

Music should build toward the final hero shot without overpowering the voiceover.

EDITING RHYTHM:
Start slow and mysterious.
Build tension during the product reveal.
Increase cutting speed during the detail sequence.
Briefly slow down for the final reveal.
Finish with a powerful clean hero frame.

FINAL HERO:
End on an immaculate low three-quarter hero shot of the exact same burgundy sandal centered on the runway.

The sandal is illuminated by a controlled overhead spotlight while its reflection appears on the glossy floor.

Camera performs a very subtle final push-in.

Display:

“THE NEW ICON”

Then a clean fade to black.

OVERALL FEEL:
Ultra-premium.
Futuristic.
Fashion-forward.
Minimal.
Cinematic.
Sophisticated.
Commercially believable.
Like a multimillion-dollar luxury footwear campaign.

STRICTLY AVOID:
Product morphing, changing sandal design, changing colors, additional footwear, humans, feet, distorted anatomy, duplicated products, floating objects, random props, excessive VFX, cartoon effects, cheap transitions, illegible typography, flickering text, warped geometry, unrealistic leather, or inconsistent hardware.
