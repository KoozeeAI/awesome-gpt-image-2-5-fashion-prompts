The hero products are the NOCTELLE "VELVET STEP HEEL" and "PRESSLINE TIGHTS". The style is clean white-studio fashion photography with a reduced, asymmetrical media setup: only a few cropped cameras and microphones enter the frame, all directing attention to the model’s legs and black satin heel without visual chaos.

One leg bears weight; the other bends upward so the black heel, ankle strap, and sheer tights become the main readable product zone. The model wears a minimal black one-shoulder mini dress. Her lifted leg creates a diagonal from lower left toward center, placing the shoe near the lower-left focal area. One hand hovers near the lifted shoe without touching it; the other rests naturally along the dress. Her head turns slightly toward the right-side microphones, expression calm and editorial.

Hero products: NOCTELLE "VELVET STEP HEEL", a black satin low-heel slingback with a pointed almond toe, slim elastic back strap, subtle curved vamp, and small sculpted heel; paired with NOCTELLE "PRESSLINE TIGHTS", sheer black tights with even transparency, smooth knee tension, and soft matte finish. The shoe must show controlled satin highlights, crisp edges, and a clean heel silhouette. The tights must reveal natural skin tone through dark sheer fabric, especially at the knee, calf, and ankle.

The media layout is sparse and intentionally uneven: one large cropped TV camera enters from the upper-left foreground, two microphones enter from the right at different heights and angles, and one smaller camera appears at the lower right aimed upward toward the shoe. The upper-left camera must not cover the face or lifted leg. The microphones stop near the shoulder and upper torso line, not parallel. The lower-right camera reinforces product focus. Microphone flags carry the original text "NOCTELLE PRESS" in small white sans serif on black foam. No device overlaps the shoe, tights, face, or bottom wordmark.

Pure white seamless studio background with a soft floor contact shadow beneath the grounded foot. No furniture, no street setting, no extra props beyond the four media devices. Depth comes from the cropped left camera, right microphones, lower-right camera, and central model. The composition must stay clean, editorial, and controlled.

Soft high-key studio lighting with a gentle upper-left key and frontal fill. Black dress, heels, tights, microphones, and cameras stay separated by subtle edge highlights and tonal differences, never collapsing into one black mass. Color hierarchy: white background as the negative field, black fashion products and press equipment as the graphic structure, natural skin tone visible through the tights, small white microphone labels, and the bottom black wordmark as typographic anchor.

Highest detail belongs to the footwear and hosiery: satin heel sheen, almond toe curve, elastic slingback strap, small heel shape, tights transparency over knee and calf, fabric tension, ankle contour, and realistic skin-through-fabric transition. The black mini dress stays simple and secondary. Cameras and microphones show believable lenses, grips, hands, foam heads, and cables, but remain slightly less sharp than the shoe and leg area. Typography is crisp and flat.
