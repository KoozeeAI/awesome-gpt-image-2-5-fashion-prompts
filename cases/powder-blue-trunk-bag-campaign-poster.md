## Prompt

A full-bleed vertical luxury handbag poster for the original resort leather brand CIELOVA, merging wind-driven coastal narrative with precise trunk-bag craftsmanship. The hero product is the CIELOVA "SKYLOCK TRUNK", a powder-blue structured micro trunk bag suspended at the center of a low-angle fashion-commercial scene. The image combines realistic sunlit fashion photography, flat red-and-teal architectural color blocking, clean sky negative space, and sparse luxury typography.

Extreme low-angle viewpoint from the terrace floor, looking upward from below waist height. The bag hangs in the lower-center foreground, close enough for the lock, corner plates, strap hardware, leather grain, and stitched panel edges to read clearly. The model, legs, and red geometric slabs rise around it, creating monumental scale without reducing product readability.

Two tall red sculptural slabs stand at the left and right edges, with cool teal side planes angled inward to form a triangular gateway. The adult model bends forward from the upper right, one leg extended left and the other planted right, creating a dynamic arch over the suspended bag. The pale-blue strap curves from the foreground hand down to the central lock, guiding the eye into the product. The sky remains open for typography; no text overlaps the bag, face, hands, or lock.

Hero product: CIELOVA "SKYLOCK TRUNK", a small structured powder-blue leather trunk bag with a rigid rectangular box body, fine pebble-grain surface, brushed-gold corner plates, polished gold rivets, central gold lock clasp, short top handle, detachable pale-blue shoulder strap, and precise stitched panel edges. The front lock plate carries an original CIELOVA emblem: a minimal gold arched-horizon mark above the small uppercase word "CIELOVA". The bag must feel symmetrical, tactile, sharply edged, and commercially credible.

An adult female fashion model, clearly over 20, wears an ivory sleeveless resort bodysuit with a narrow black waist band and glossy burgundy pointed heels. She leans forward in a stable crouched stance, one hand gripping the pale-blue strap near the camera, the other controlling the hanging bag. Her gaze points downward toward the viewer with a composed, intent expression. Wind pushes her hair diagonally from upper right toward left, echoing the strap movement.

The handbag hangs between the model’s legs and slightly in front of the torso, becoming the central point of weight and tension. The strap forms a clean curved line from hand to lock clasp; the gold rings and corner plates catch the same warm rim light as the shoe highlights. The red slabs sit behind the model, framing the product without intersecting the body. The pale terrace floor shows soft shoe contact shadows and a faint warm reflection below the suspended bag.

Background: a minimal coastal terrace with pale textured stone floor, low distant sea line, warm horizon glow, and clean cyan sky. The red-and-teal slabs are simple hard-edged matte volumes. Depth comes from the low horizon, slab scale, leg spacing, strap overlap, and foreground product detail. No extra buildings, crowds, plants, vehicles, jewelry clutter, or decorative props.

Late-afternoon backlight comes from low behind the model, creating warm rim highlights along the legs, hair, bag edges, lock clasp, rivets, and gold corner plates. Soft frontal fill keeps the bag’s front panel, emblem, stitching, leather grain, and lock readable. Color hierarchy: powder blue bag and sky as the cool core, deep red slabs and burgundy heels as accents, ivory bodysuit and pale stone as neutral support, brushed gold hardware as precise highlight points. Shadows stay warm and transparent, never dead black.

Highest detail belongs to the handbag: rigid trunk geometry, pebble leather grain, brushed-gold protectors, clasp depth, rivet reflections, seam shadows, strap thickness, hardware tension, and believable hanging gravity. Skin and clothing are realistic but secondary. Hair has clear wind direction and separated edges. The slabs remain smooth and graphic. Product edges and typography must be crisp.

Exact visible text only:
"UNLOCK THE SKY"
"CIELOVA"
"SKYLOCK TRUNK"
"RESORT LEATHER GOODS · COASTAL EDITION"

Typography: refined geometric sans serif with wide letter spacing, white or pale ivory. "UNLOCK THE SKY" sits large in the upper sky negative space above the model’s shoulder line. "CIELOVA" sits smaller beneath it. "SKYLOCK TRUNK" and the auxiliary line align in the lower-right negative space above the terrace line. Maintain clear margins.

Full-bleed vertical commercial poster.
