# Y2K Luxury Sneaker Campaign Editorial

A curated AI fashion prompt for Shoes · Product-centered Y2K sneaker campaign with coordinated full outfit and showroom styling · Image.

## Prompt

GPT Image 2.5 on ChatGPT

Prompt:

Create a high-end, ultra-realistic full-body Y2K fashion editorial photograph of a young woman with a cute, playful aesthetic, photographed in a professional white studio.

CHARACTER:
Young woman with a short, softly tousled wavy lavender-purple bob haircut, wispy bangs, and several tiny pastel butterfly and flower hair clips. Fair natural skin with subtle freckles across the nose and cheeks, soft youthful facial features, natural pink lips, delicate eyeliner, and fresh minimal makeup. Her appearance should feel authentic, charming, and naturally photogenic.

OUTFIT:
She wears an oversized cream-white cropped sweatshirt featuring a colorful retro Y2K graphic with tiny stars, butterflies, and abstract pastel shapes. Pair it with a pleated pastel-lilac mini skirt layered over subtle white shorts, sheer white tights decorated with tiny embroidered bows, and chunky futuristic white sneakers with translucent soles, holographic silver panels, pastel mint details, and vivid neon-pink laces. Add a stack of colorful pastel scrunchie bracelets and a few cute Y2K-inspired rings.

POSE:
She is sitting casually on the glossy white studio floor with one knee raised toward the camera and the other leg folded comfortably underneath her. Her torso leans slightly toward the raised leg while she holds the sneaker with both hands, adjusting and tightening the neon-pink laces. Her head is turned slightly toward the camera rather than directly downward, with a small playful smile and relaxed expression. The foreground sneaker appears larger due to natural perspective, creating a strong fashion-editorial composition.

COMPOSITION:
Full-body vertical fashion photograph, 4:5 aspect ratio. Camera positioned low at floor level with a slightly wide perspective, while maintaining realistic proportions. The woman remains the clear central subject. Use natural body anatomy, realistic hands and fingers, authentic fabric folds, realistic skin texture, and physically accurate shadows.

SET DESIGN:
Transform the white studio into a sophisticated Y2K sneaker showroom. Place pastel pink, lavender, and mint shoeboxes around her in asymmetrical stacks. Add a small transparent acrylic display stand holding an extra pair of futuristic sneakers, a compact sneaker-cleaning kit, pastel brushes, loose neon shoelaces, tiny keychains, glossy stickers, and scattered metallic star accessories.

BACKGROUND GRAPHICS:
Instead of large text covering the background, use subtle hand-drawn Y2K graphics integrated into the studio walls: tiny hearts, stars, butterflies, smiley faces, cherries, shooting stars, lightning bolts, planets, sparkles, and sneaker outlines. Add a few small handwritten-style labels such as “KICK IT,” “Y2K LOVE,” and “SO FRESH” as tasteful decorative elements.

LIGHTING:
Bright premium fashion-studio lighting with a large softbox as the key light, gentle fill light, and subtle rim lighting separating her purple hair from the white background. Clean soft shadows underneath her body and sneakers. Slight glossy reflections on the studio floor. Pastel colors should remain vibrant but sophisticated.

PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE:
Ultra-realistic commercial fashion photography, luxury sneaker campaign, early-2000s-inspired editorial aesthetic, contemporary magazine photography, realistic skin pores and fine hair strands, detailed fabric texture, crisp sneaker materials, subtle film grain, natural depth of field, realistic reflections, high dynamic range, professional color grading, extremely detailed, photorealistic, 8K quality.

IMPORTANT:
No distorted anatomy, no extra fingers, no malformed shoes, no duplicated objects, no plastic-looking skin, no excessive blur, no artificial-looking face, no overly exaggerated proportions, no cartoon rendering. The final image should look like an authentic professionally photographed Y2K sneaker campaign rather than an AI-generated image.
